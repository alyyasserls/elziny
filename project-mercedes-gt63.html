Mercedes-AMG GT 63

AMG · GT 63 · 4-Door Coupe · Mercedes-Benz

AMG aggression stretched into a full four-door grand tourer.

This GT 63 carries everything people expect from the AMG 4-Door Coupe silhouette: the long hood, the Panamericana grille, and the low, wide stance that makes the car feel more GT than sedan. Finished in red with yellow brake calipers and a vivid red-and-black interior, it leans fully into the dramatic side of Mercedes-AMG rather than trying to hide it.

Ambient-Lit Cockpit

The interior image shows exactly where the GT 63 separates itself from a standard performance sedan. The sweeping dashboard, turbine vents, carbon trim, and ambient lighting turn the cabin into something theatrical, while the red-and-black color split keeps the overall spec feeling sharp and unmistakably AMG.

GT 63 Identity

The rear badge detail keeps the message direct. This is not just a Mercedes with a strong spec. It is a GT 63, which means the car sits in that narrow space between executive comfort and full AMG intensity. The proportions, the lighting, and the badging all support that identity without needing anything extra.
