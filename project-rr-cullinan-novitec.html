Rolls-Royce Cullinan Novitec

V12 Twin-Turbo · Novitec · Ultra-Luxury SUV · 2024

Bespoke opulence with a more defiant silhouette.

This Cullinan wears Novitec treatment with unusual confidence, pairing Rolls-Royce scale and ceremony with a more assertive visual package. The white exterior, dark wheels, and revised lower bodywork change the tone immediately, while the open coach door reveals an orange-and-ivory interior that feels more couture than automotive. It is still unmistakably a Rolls-Royce, but one aimed at a client who wanted the standard Cullinan to say more.

Engine 6.75L Twin-Turbo V12

Top Speed 250 km/h
Conversion Novitec Styling Package
Interior Ivory & Orange Leather · Starlight Headliner
Drive All-Wheel Drive
Exterior White with Black and Carbon Accents

Bespoke Front Lounge

The front cabin is where the Cullinan Novitec reveals its real personality. Ivory leather meets a saturated orange contrast treatment across the dashboard, doors, steering wheel, and center console, all beneath the signature Starlight headliner. It feels bright, handcrafted, and unmistakably tailored to a specific client rather than built to a standard spec sheet.

Craft in the Smallest Details

Even the audio speaker treatment shows why Rolls-Royce cabins feel different from everything else. The polished Bespoke Audio grille is treated like jewelry, not hardware. In a car like this, those details matter just as much as the powertrain or the exterior stance, because the real experience is built from the parts most owners touch and notice every day.
