Mercedes-AMG S63 E Performance

V8 Biturbo Hybrid · 791 HP · AMG Performance Sedan · 2024

Executive luxury, rewritten by AMG.

The S63 E Performance takes the full comfort and ceremony of the S-Class and injects it with flagship AMG violence. Finished in gloss black with turbine-style multi-spoke wheels and a vivid red cabin, it pairs a handcrafted 4.0-litre V8 biturbo with hybrid assistance for immense, effortless performance. This is not merely a luxury sedan. It is the top floor of the AMG empire.

Engine 4.0L V8 Biturbo Hybrid
Power 791 HP
0–100 km/h 3.3 seconds
Top Speed 290 km/h
Drive AMG Performance 4MATIC+
Interior Red Nappa Leather · Ambient Lighting
Transmission AMG SPEEDSHIFT MCT 9G
Exterior Gloss Black

Driver-Focused Command Center

The front cabin balances traditional S-Class opulence with unmistakable AMG intent. The steering wheel, performance controls, digital cluster, and ambient lighting create a cockpit that feels technical and indulgent at the same time. It is a car designed to isolate you from the outside world while still reminding you that 791 horsepower sits under your right foot.

First-Class Rear Lounge

The rear compartment is where the S63 separates itself from ordinary performance sedans. Diamond-stitched red leather, reclining comfort, and executive amenities turn the back seat into a private lounge. It delivers AMG speed, but it never forgets that the S-Class is still meant to be experienced from every seat.
