Aston Martin DBS Superleggera

DBS Superleggera · Aston Martin · Grand Tourer · Black

Low, elegant, and unmistakably British in character.

This Aston Martin DBS Superleggera brings a different tone to the collection than the harder-edged performance cars around it. The exterior relies on proportion rather than visual aggression, with a long hood, broad grille, and low roofline doing most of the work. Inside, the oxblood leather cabin shifts the experience toward classic grand-touring luxury, where texture and craftsmanship matter as much as the silhouette itself.

Handcrafted GT Cabin

The interior photo does most of the selling here. The color alone changes the feel of the car immediately, but the stitching, seat quilting, and layered center stack also give it a richness that feels tailored instead of mass-produced. It is a cabin made to slow the experience down rather than turn everything into a digital display.

Quiet Luxury

The seat detail shows exactly why Aston Martin interiors feel distinct. The leather, embossing, and subtle logo work are all deliberate without becoming flashy. That restraint suits the car as a whole. It is elegant first, fast second, and that balance is what gives it lasting appeal.
