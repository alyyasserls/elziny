Lamborghini Huracán STO

V10 Naturally Aspirated · 640 HP · RWD · Super Trofeo Omologata · 2024

Sharper, lighter, and stripped down to intent.

This Huracán STO appears in satin silver with contrasting yellow details, giving Lamborghini's most focused V10 road car an even more surgical look. The long front clamshell, central hood vents, and motorsport stance make its purpose obvious before the engine even starts. It is still the same 5.2-litre naturally aspirated V10 sending 640 HP to the rear wheels, but in this specification the visual message is colder, cleaner, and every bit as aggressive.

Engine 5.2L V10 Naturally Aspirated
Power 640 HP @ 8,000 rpm
0–100 km/h 3.0 seconds
Top Speed 310 km/h
Drive RWD — Rear-Wheel Drive
Interior Alcantara · Yellow Stitching · Carbon Fibre
Transmission 7-Speed LDF Dual-Clutch
Exterior Satin Silver with Yellow Accents

Race Bodywork Exposed

With the front clamshell lifted, the STO's engineering becomes part of the theatre. The shape is pure function: cooling, downforce, and airflow management taken directly from Lamborghini's competition cars. It is one of the rare road cars that looks even more dramatic mid-service position than it does parked closed.

Minimal Cabin, Maximum Focus

The cockpit stays loyal to the STO brief. Alcantara wraps nearly every major touchpoint, yellow details trace the dashboard and steering wheel, and the center console keeps the red start switch front and center. It feels more like strapping into an event than stepping into a conventional road car.
