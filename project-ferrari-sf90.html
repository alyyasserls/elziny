Ferrari SF90 Stradale

V8 Twin-Turbo Hybrid · 1,000 HP · Spider · 2024

One thousand horsepower. One Ferrari.

The SF90 Stradale is the most powerful road car Ferrari has ever produced — a 4.0-litre twin-turbocharged V8 joined by three electric motors to deliver a combined 1,000 HP. In Rosso Corsa with an aluminium centre stripe, it commands the room before it moves a metre. 0–100 km/h in 2.5 seconds. A top speed of 340 km/h. And behind it all, the unmistakable Prancing Horse — a symbol that has never meant more than it does right now.

Engine 4.0L V8 Twin-Turbo Hybrid
Power 1,000 HP
0–100 km/h 2.5 seconds
Top Speed 340 km/h
Body Style Spider
Interior Black & Red Alcantara
Transmission 8-Speed Dual-Clutch
Exterior Rosso Corsa · Aluminium Stripe

One Thousand Reasons

The twin-turbocharged V8 sits mid-mounted, its red cam covers stamped with the Ferrari name — a detail only those who truly know will ever see. Every component is machined, assembled, and tested by hand in Maranello. This engine is not built. It is crafted.

The Prancing Horse.

Seen from behind, the SF90 Spider reveals its carbon fibre bodywork and the single illuminated LED bar that spans the tail — a signature as unmistakable as the Prancing Horse badge it carries. This is how Ferrari says goodbye as it disappears into the distance.
