Lamborghini Huracán EVO Spyder

V10 Naturally Aspirated · 640 HP · Convertible Spyder · 2024

V10 fury, completely unfiltered.

The Huracán EVO Spyder takes the emotional intensity of Lamborghini's legendary 5.2-litre naturally aspirated V10 and strips away the roof. What remains is a raw, mechanical connection between driver, machine, and the elements. Wrapped in Nero Noctis black with a striking blue Alcantara and leather interior over forged carbon fibre, this EVO Spyder looks dangerous even at a standstill. 640 horsepower, 0–100 km/h in 3.1 seconds, and an exhaust note that commands complete attention.

Engine 5.2L V10 Naturally Aspirated
Power 640 HP @ 8,000 rpm
0–100 km/h 3.1 seconds
Top Speed 325 km/h
Body Style Soft-Top Spyder
Interior Blue & Black Alcantara over Carbon Fibre
Transmission 7-Speed Dual-Clutch
Exterior Nero Noctis

Designed Around the Driver

The command centre of the EVO Spyder leaves no room for distraction. Vibrant blue leather accents frame the digital instrument cluster and massive paddle shifters, while the central touchscreen system handles everything from telephony to Lamborghini Dinamica Veicolo Integrata (LDVI) telemetry. The fighter-jet style ignition cover guards the button that brings all ten cylinders to life.

Carbon Fibre Core

Behind the striking two-tone sport seats lies the structural integrity of a supercar. Forged composite carbon fibre wraps around the carbon-fibre bucket seats, lowering the centre of gravity while locking the driver securely in place. Exposed carbon bulkheads sit just inches from the screaming V10 sitting right over your shoulder.
