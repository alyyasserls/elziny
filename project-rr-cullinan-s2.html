Rolls-Royce Cullinan

Series II · V12 Twin-Turbo · 600 HP · 2025

Evolved. Reimagined. Absolute.

The Cullinan Series II represents a profound evolution of Rolls-Royce's most adventurous creation. A commanding black exterior opens to reveal a breathtaking turquoise leather interior — a bespoke commission that transforms every journey into an event. Beneath the bonnet, the legendary 6.75-litre twin-turbocharged V12 remains, delivering 600 horsepower with the characteristic silence and effortlessness only a Rolls-Royce can achieve.

Engine 6.75L V12 Twin-Turbo
Power 600 HP @ 5,000 rpm
0–100 km/h 4.9 seconds
Top Speed 250 km/h Limited
Body Style Ultra-Luxury SUV
Interior Bespoke Turquoise Leather
Transmission 8-Speed Automatic (ZF)
Exterior Black · Series II

A Name Cast in Light

The illuminated Cullinan nameplate — a Rolls-Royce signature — glows with quiet authority. Named after the largest gem-quality rough diamond ever discovered, the Cullinan wears its identity as effortlessly as everything else it does.

The Heart of Silence

The hand-assembled 6.75-litre V12 is a masterpiece of controlled power. 900 Nm of torque arrives almost instantaneously, yet the cabin remains hushed — a contradiction only Rolls-Royce engineers have ever truly mastered. This engine does not roar. It glides.
