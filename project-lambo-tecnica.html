Lamborghini Huracán Tecnica

V10 Naturally Aspirated · 640 HP · RWD Coupe · 2024

Pure. Unfiltered. Rear-wheel.

The Huracán Tecnica is Lamborghini's most driver-focused Huracán ever made — stripping everything back to what matters. A 5.2-litre naturally aspirated V10 screaming to 8,000 rpm, channelling 640 HP through the rear wheels alone. In Rosso Efesto, it ignites the room. Inside, dark Alcantara sport seats with crimson stitching and exposed carbon fibre tell you exactly what this car is built for. 0–100 km/h in 3.2 seconds. An icon that demands you feel every metre.

Engine 5.2L V10 Naturally Aspirated
Power 640 HP @ 8,000 rpm
0–100 km/h 3.2 seconds
Top Speed 325 km/h
Drive RWD — Rear-Wheel Drive
Interior Dark Alcantara · Red Stitching
Transmission 7-Speed LDF Dual-Clutch
Exterior Rosso Efesto

Born to Drive Rear

Unlike its all-wheel-drive siblings, the Tecnica sends all 640 horsepower purely through the rear axle — demanding precision, rewarding commitment. The result is a supercar that communicates every nuance of the road directly to your hands, making every drive feel earned.

A Cockpit Without Compromise

Slide into the Tecnica's cabin and every surface tells the same story. Dark Alcantara sport seats with bold crimson stitching and embossed Lamborghini crests, flanked by exposed carbon fibre structural elements. This is an interior that means business — and looks extraordinary doing it.
