Range Rover Autobiography

V8 Twin-Turbo · 530 HP · Luxury SUV · 2024

Quiet authority in a softer, more understated shade.

This Range Rover Autobiography arrives in a satin-style grey finish that gives the latest body shape a calmer, more architectural presence. The exterior stays restrained and clean, while the interior shifts the tone to warmth with a beige leather cabin, open-pore wood, and the latest floating-screen layout. It delivers the same flagship Range Rover formula, but with a more muted and elegant visual identity.

Engine 4.4L V8 Twin-Turbo
Power 530 HP
0–100 km/h 4.6 seconds
Top Speed 250 km/h
Drive All-Wheel Drive
Interior Beige Leather · Wood Veneer · Curved Display
Body Style Full-Size Luxury SUV
Exterior Grey

Modern Range Rover Interior

The front cabin shows exactly why the latest Range Rover feels so different from previous generations. The dash is calmer, cleaner, and more architectural, with the touchscreen and climate interface integrated into a layout that feels more like premium furniture than traditional automotive design. In beige and wood trim, the effect is especially warm and refined.

Minimalist Rear Signature

The rear three-quarter angle captures the latest Range Rover design language at its best: clean vertical taillights, strong surfacing, and almost no visual clutter. It is a shape designed to feel expensive from every angle, and in this grey finish the surfaces and proportions do most of the talking.
