Lamborghini Urus S Novitec

V8 Twin-Turbo · Novitec · Super SUV · Lamborghini

Stealth, scale, and tuner aggression in one silhouette.

This Lamborghini Urus S wears its Novitec identity the right way: fully blacked-out, low-slung, and visibly more assertive without losing the shape that made the Urus instantly recognizable. The exterior shot sets the tone with a satin-style dark finish, oversized black wheels, and red brake hardware, while the engine bay and exhaust details confirm the deeper Novitec character underneath. It is still a Lamborghini first, but one sharpened through a darker, more technical lens.

Novitec Under the Skin

The engine bay detail makes the build clear immediately. Lamborghini V8 architecture remains at the center, but the Novitec marking places this Urus S in a different category from the standard showroom car. It is a stronger statement for buyers who want the Urus platform with tuner credibility already built into the package.

Dark Signature

The rear exhaust detail distills the whole car into one frame: minimal light, deep surfaces, and a small Novitec script that says enough on its own. This is the kind of finish that works because it does not over-explain itself. Everything is black, everything is sharp, and every visible part supports the same hard-edged visual identity.
