Ferrari 296 GTB

V6 Hybrid · 830 HP · Berlinetta · 2024

Compact Ferrari architecture, amplified by hybrid force.

This 296 GTB arrives in black with a bold yellow center livery, turning Ferrari's compact berlinetta into something even sharper visually. Beneath the body sits a twin-turbo V6 paired with electric assistance for a combined 830 HP, delivering a car that feels smaller, lighter, and more immediate than its numbers already suggest. It is a modern Ferrari with old-school visual drama and new-school brutality.

Engine 3.0L Twin-Turbo V6 Hybrid
Power 830 HP
0–100 km/h 2.9 seconds
Top Speed 330 km/h
Drive RWD
Interior Carbon Fibre · Digital Passenger Display
Transmission 8-Speed Dual-Clutch
Exterior Black with Yellow Livery

Hybrid Heart, Ferrari Theatre

The rear section reveals the engineering philosophy of the 296 GTB. Ferrari's compact hybrid packaging, exposed with the engine bay open, shows just how tightly the V6, electrification hardware, and exhaust architecture have been integrated. The result is not merely efficiency. It is a new style of Ferrari performance built around immediacy and precision.

Minimalism With Intent

The cabin detail keeps the 296 GTB clean and technical, with carbon trim, slim digital surfaces, and the 296 GTB signature integrated into the dashboard architecture. It is a restrained, almost architectural interior treatment, which fits the car's lighter and more compact philosophy compared with Ferrari's larger front-engined machines.
