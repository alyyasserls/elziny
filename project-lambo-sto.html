Lamborghini Huracán STO

V10 Naturally Aspirated · 640 HP · RWD · Super Trofeo Omologata · 2024

Born on the track. Cleared for the road.

The Huracán STO — Super Trofeo Omologata — is the most extreme street-legal Huracán ever conceived. Sixty percent of its components are shared directly with the Huracán Super Trofeo race car. The 5.2-litre naturally aspirated V10 screams to 8,000 rpm, delivering 640 HP through the rear wheels alone. In Oro Elios gold beneath purple-lit showroom air, this is a car that belongs in a museum — but demands to be driven flat-out.

Engine 5.2L V10 Naturally Aspirated
Power 640 HP @ 8,000 rpm
0–100 km/h 3.0 seconds
Top Speed 310 km/h
Drive RWD — Rear-Wheel Drive
Interior Alcantara · Gold Stitching · Carbon Fibre
Transmission 7-Speed LDF Dual-Clutch
Exterior Oro Elios (Gold)

A Race Pilot's Instrument

Inside the STO, there is no ambiguity. The Alcantara-wrapped steering wheel sits low, gold accent stitching threads through every surface, and the instrument cluster fires up like a race car at the start line. The red engine-start button on the column paddle is not decoration — it is a command. Every element is precisely placed, purposefully minimal, and uncompromisingly focused.

Ten Cylinders of Pure Intent

Lift the polycarbonate engine cover and the 5.2-litre V10 is revealed in all its raw, unfiltered glory — dressed in gold cam covers that mirror the exterior, flanked by a lightweight roll hoop finished in gloss carbon. No turbos. No hybrid assistance. Just 640 horses pulling freely to 8,000 rpm, each one earned by engineering alone.
