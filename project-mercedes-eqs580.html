Mercedes EQS 580 SUV

Dual-Motor Electric · Luxury SUV · 4MATIC · 2024

Silence, scale, and full electric presence.

The EQS 580 SUV replaces theatrical exhaust note with something more modern: instant torque, near-silent progress, and a sense of weightless luxury. Finished in black with the signature illuminated EQ front treatment, it leans into the smoother, more futuristic side of Mercedes design while preserving the comfort and ceremony expected from the top end of the lineup.

Powertrain Dual-Motor Electric
Drive 4MATIC All-Wheel Drive
0–100 km/h 4.6 seconds
Range Up to 600 km (WLTP)
Body Style Full-Size Luxury EV SUV
Interior Ambient Lighting · Executive Rear Console
Charging DC Fast Charging
Exterior Black

Ambient-Lit Driver Zone

The front cabin leans fully into Mercedes' modern EV design language: broad digital surfaces, soft materials, and a vivid strip of ambient light tracing the doors and dash. The feel is less mechanical than a traditional AMG product and more lounge-like, which suits the EQS brief perfectly.

Rear Comfort by Design

The rear console detail shows where the EQS 580 SUV earns its flagship positioning. The focus is on comfort, access, and ease rather than visual noise, turning the back seat into a proper place to spend time. It is an electric SUV built to feel premium from every row, not just the front seats.
