Tesla Cybertruck

Tri-Motor Electric · 845 HP · Beast Mode · 2024

The future, delivered.

Nothing on the road looks, moves, or thinks like the Tesla Cybertruck. An ultra-hard stainless steel exoskeleton wraps three electric motors producing a combined 845 HP — enough to launch this six-foot machine to 100 km/h in just 2.6 seconds. The cabin is a minimalist command centre: a single 18.5-inch display, yoke-style steering, and a silence that makes the acceleration feel almost impossible. This is not a truck. It is a statement about what comes next.

Minimal. Total.

Inside, every convention is discarded. The Cybertruck's cabin strips everything back to a single 18.5-inch touchscreen commanding all vehicle functions. No buttons. No noise. No compromise. Just the road ahead and 845 horses waiting for your input.

Built Different.

The angular stainless steel bed with its signature red LED light bar leaves no doubt — this machine was not designed to blend in. The Cybertruck graffiti script across the tailgate is a provocation. A declaration. An icon that owns every space it occupies.
