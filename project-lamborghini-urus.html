Lamborghini Urus

V8 Twin-Turbo · 666 HP · Super SUV · 2024

The world's first Super SUV.

The Lamborghini Urus redefines what an SUV can be — a 666 HP twin-turbocharged V8, wrapped in an all-black Nero Noctis finish with exposed carbon fibre detailing throughout. Inside, the cockpit-inspired interior places Anima drive mode selection at the driver's fingertips, while red accents cut through the darkness like a pulse. Raw. Relentless. Unstoppable.

Engine 4.0L V8 Twin-Turbo
Power 666 HP
0–100 km/h 3.3 seconds
Top Speed 306 km/h
Body Style Super SUV
Interior Black & Red Alcantara
Transmission 8-Speed Automatic
Exterior Nero Noctis

Anima in Command

Five drive modes — Strada, Sport, Corsa, Neve, and the track-focused Anima — give the Urus a personality for every road. The driver-focused cockpit wraps you in rich red Alcantara and carbon, with every control precisely placed for maximum engagement. The Lamborghini script runs across the dashboard as both a reminder and a promise.

Carbon Obsession

Every surface tells a story of obsessive craftsmanship. Forged carbon fibre panels embossed with the Lamborghini nameplate run through the interior, a detail only those who truly know will recognise. Dark. Textured. Uncompromising — in every dimension.
