Lamborghini Revuelto

V12 Hybrid · 1001 HP · Coupe · 2024

The first HPEV hybrid super sports car. A 6.5L V12 combined with three electric motors producing a staggering 1001 HP. Finished in a striking Viola exterior with carbon fiber details.

The Heartbeat of the HPEV

The Revuelto combines a naturally aspirated 6.5-liter V12 engine screaming to 9,250 RPM with three electric motors. The result is an unprecedented hybrid super sports car that delivers a staggering 1001 horsepower and an explosive linear power delivery.

A Pilot’s Cabin

Step inside and the Revuelto wraps you in luxurious Italian craftsmanship with state-of-the-art exposed carbon fiber components. It introduces a totally new human-machine interface designed firmly around the pilot for ultimate control and engagement at hyper speeds.
