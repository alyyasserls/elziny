Mercedes-AMG SL63

V8 Biturbo · 585 HP · Roadster · 2024

Grand touring elegance, rearmed by AMG.

The SL63 brings back one of Mercedes' most iconic nameplates with a harder edge. Finished in gloss black with a vivid red cockpit, it pairs handcrafted AMG V8 power with a retractable roof and a long-hood roadster silhouette that still feels unmistakably SL. It is a luxury convertible first, but the grille, stance, and soundtrack make it clear that AMG had the final word.

Engine 4.0L V8 Biturbo
Power 585 HP
0–100 km/h 3.6 seconds
Top Speed 315 km/h
Drive AMG Performance 4MATIC+
Interior Red Nappa Leather · Ambient Lighting
Transmission AMG SPEEDSHIFT MCT 9G
Exterior Gloss Black

Open-Top Command Center

The SL63 cockpit mixes familiar Mercedes luxury with a more intimate, performance-led layout. The upright center display, turbine vents, AMG wheel, and red leather trim make the cabin feel theatrical at night and purposeful during the day. It is a roadster interior that still remembers how to be an AMG.

Handcrafted AMG V8

Under the hood sits AMG's familiar 4.0-litre biturbo V8, hand-built and signed, delivering the effortless torque and instant response expected from the badge. In the SL63, that engine does more than provide speed. It gives the car its character, turning a refined grand tourer into something far more alive.
