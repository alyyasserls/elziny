Mercedes-Maybach S580

V8 Biturbo · 503 HP · 2-Tone Maybach · 2024

Limousine luxury with Maybach ceremony in every surface.

This Mercedes-Maybach S580 arrives in a two-tone black and champagne-gold finish that immediately separates it from the standard S-Class. Long, low, and formal, it carries the visual weight expected of a true chauffeur sedan while keeping the modern Maybach grille and wheel design front and center. Inside, the atmosphere shifts into lounge territory, with deep ambient lighting and rear-seat comfort treated as the real focal point.

Engine 4.0L V8 Biturbo
Power 503 HP
0–100 km/h 4.8 seconds
Top Speed 250 km/h
Drive 4MATIC
Interior Executive Rear Lounge · Ambient Lighting
Body Style Luxury Chauffeur Sedan
Exterior 2-Tone Black and Champagne Gold

Ambient-Lit Executive Cabin

The cabin trades overt aggression for controlled theatre. Blue ambient lighting wraps the dashboard and doors, while the upright center display and rear-first seating position make it clear the S580 is designed to be experienced as much from the back as from behind the wheel. It is less a cockpit than a private suite with a steering wheel attached.

Maybach Identity in the Details

The instrument display and trim details remind you this is not a regular S-Class wearing extra chrome. The Maybach graphics, lighting palette, and restrained presentation are all part of the experience. It is a car engineered around composure, status, and the sense that nothing in the cabin was left ordinary.
