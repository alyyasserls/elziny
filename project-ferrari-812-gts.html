Ferrari 812 Novitec

V12 · 6.5L · Novitec · 1 of 1 in Egypt · Ferrari

This is the real Ferrari 812 Novitec now shown in the collection: black over a vivid turquoise-accented cabin, marked by a matching center stripe and presented as a 1-of-1 car in Egypt. The Novitec identity adds another layer of rarity to an already special front-engine V12 Ferrari, giving the car a sharper, more technical visual signature while keeping the unmistakable long-hood 812 presence intact.

Novitec V12 Presence

The engine bay photo says what the exterior only hints at. This is still the 6.5-liter naturally aspirated Ferrari V12 layout enthusiasts care about, but now framed as a Novitec build rather than a standard showroom 812. The result feels less like a catalog car and more like a specific statement piece chosen for rarity as much as performance.

One of One

The turquoise interior treatment is what makes the car instantly memorable. Against the black exterior, the color reads almost like jewelry rather than trim, and it connects directly back to the stripe on the hood. That continuity is what makes the spec feel intentional. If this really is the only one in Egypt, the cabin is the part no one forgets after seeing it once.
