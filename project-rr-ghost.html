Rolls-Royce Ghost

V12 Twin-Turbo · 563 HP · Luxury Sedan · 2024

Presence without noise.

The Ghost is the Rolls-Royce for the client who wants the full measure of craftsmanship and engineering without needing to announce it. Finished in white with polished chrome detailing, it carries the formal architecture of the marque in a cleaner, more contemporary form. The effect is less theatrical than a Cullinan and more intimate than a Phantom, but no less imposing once it settles into a room.

Engine 6.75L Twin-Turbo V12
Power 563 HP
0–100 km/h 4.8 seconds
Top Speed 250 km/h
Transmission 8-Speed Automatic
Interior Ivory Leather · Wood Veneer · Piano Black
Drive All-Wheel Drive
Exterior White with Chrome Accents

Ivory Lounge Atmosphere

The cabin is where the Ghost makes its strongest case. Ivory leather wraps the seats and lower surfaces, while dark trim and polished metal keep the environment formal rather than overly soft. The dashboard architecture is deliberately horizontal and calm, built to reduce visual stress and make every switch, vent, and surface feel expensive without appearing crowded.

Signature Details Up Close

Even the wheel detailing reflects the Ghost’s character. The polished surfaces, restrained contrast, and RR-branded center treatment mirror the car’s overall philosophy: luxury expressed through precision, not exaggeration. It is this consistency in the smaller components that gives a Ghost its unmistakable sense of quality.
