Ferrari F8

Coupe · Ferrari · F8 · Mid-Engine

A modern Ferrari berlinetta with enough visual aggression to speak for itself.

This Ferrari F8 uses color and contrast exactly the way a car like this should. The red exterior is sharpened by black and yellow striping, while the interior continues the same approach with deep red trim, black inserts, and yellow accents running through the seats and belts. It feels focused, immediate, and very clearly designed around drama from every angle.

Ferrari Focus

The cluster image captures the F8 mindset immediately. The tachometer dominates the view, the red lighting pushes everything toward urgency, and the surrounding display architecture stays secondary to the engine information. It is a cabin designed to keep the emotional center of the car right in front of the driver.

Color as Identity

The seat detail is what ties the whole spec together. Red Alcantara, black inserts, and a narrow yellow accent line echo the exterior livery without overcomplicating it. The result is a car that feels coordinated rather than busy, which is exactly what this kind of Ferrari spec needs.
