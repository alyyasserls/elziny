Hummer EV Edition 1

Electric Truck · Edition 1 · Supertruck · Hummer EV

Full-scale electric torque wrapped in a vehicle that still feels industrial.

The Hummer EV Edition 1 is built around mass, presence, and technical bravado. The exterior reads like a concept truck that made it to production without softening its edges, while the interior keeps the same squared-off confidence with upright screens, robust switchgear, and a steering design that looks more utility-grade than decorative. It belongs in the collection for the same reason it stands out on the road: nothing else carries this mix of electric power and military-scale attitude.

Utility, Digitized

The front cabin keeps the Hummer's identity intact without leaning on retro cues. The screens are upright and obvious, the dashboard is broad and horizontal, and every control feels designed for a vehicle that expects rough use rather than delicate handling. It is a modern EV interior, but still one with physical weight.

Edition 1 Detail

The steering wheel shot does the best job of explaining the whole truck. Thick surfaces, squared geometry, and a heavy center boss make it feel engineered first and styled second. That is exactly why the Hummer EV works visually. Even its smallest details still feel oversized.
