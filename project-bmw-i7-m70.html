BMW i7 M70

Electric Sedan · BMW · M Performance · Flagship

Electric executive presence with unmistakable BMW drama.

The BMW i7 M70 brings a different kind of luxury to the collection. The illuminated kidney grille and deep blue finish give the car a deliberate after-dark presence, while the cabin leans into comfort with rich brown leather, crystalline controls, and layered ambient light running across the dashboard. It is less about spectacle for its own sake and more about combining flagship sedan calm with M-badged intent.

Executive Lounge

The interior balances BMW's modern digital layout with the richness expected from a top-tier limousine. Brown leather, gloss black controls, and illuminated trim combine with the curved display to create a cabin that feels technical without becoming cold. It is a cleaner expression of luxury than the usual oversized sedan formula.

Night Signature

The rear detail shot captures what makes the i7 work visually. The horizontal light bar, the subtle badge, and the reflective deep blue paint give it presence without noise. It feels exact rather than aggressive, which suits a car positioned at the top of BMW's electric sedan range.
