Ferrari F8 Keyvany

V8 Twin-Turbo · 710 HP · 1 of 20 Worldwide · 2024

Rarity with an edge sharper than standard Maranello.

This Keyvany-built Ferrari F8 is one of just twenty in the world, pairing the F8 platform's twin-turbo V8 violence with a dramatically reworked visual identity. Finished in white with black-and-orange striping, exposed aero details, and a vivid red interior, it feels more like a limited-run collector piece than a regular showroom supercar. The shape is already aggressive in stock form. Keyvany pushes it into something rarer and far more theatrical.

Engine 3.9L Twin-Turbo V8
Power 710 HP
0–100 km/h 2.9 seconds
Top Speed 340 km/h
Body Mid-Engine Coupe · Keyvany Commission
Interior Rosso Leather · Carbon Fibre
Transmission 7-Speed Dual-Clutch
Production 1 of 20 Worldwide

Ferrari Drama, Redrawn

The passenger-side dashboard and cabin detailing show how the car balances bespoke exterior aggression with recognizably Ferrari interior architecture. Carbon trim, strong contrast stitching, and the deep red leather treatment create the sort of cockpit atmosphere expected from a high-spec mid-engine Ferrari, but here it serves a car with much greater rarity than usual.

Details Built for Collectors

The center console and lower cockpit details make the car feel intimate and special rather than merely fast. Carbon finishes, sculpted switchgear, and saturated Rosso leather turn every touchpoint into part of the experience. On a one-of-twenty commission, those details matter. They are what separate ownership from simply having another supercar in the garage.
