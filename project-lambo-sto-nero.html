Lamborghini Huracán STO

V10 Naturally Aspirated · 640 HP · RWD · Super Trofeo Omologata · 2024

Track-homologated aggression in a darker suit.

This Huracán STO arrives in Nero Noctis with vivid Arancio accents, turning Lamborghini's most focused V10 road car into something even more theatrical. The full-body hero shot puts the stance front and center: low, wide, and built around airflow. Underneath it all is the same naturally aspirated 5.2-litre V10 delivering 640 HP to the rear wheels alone, with motorsport DNA evident in every surface and every intake.

Engine 5.2L V10 Naturally Aspirated
Power 640 HP @ 8,000 rpm
0–100 km/h 3.0 seconds
Top Speed 310 km/h
Drive RWD — Rear-Wheel Drive
Interior Alcantara · Carbon Fibre · Arancio Accents
Transmission 7-Speed LDF Dual-Clutch
Exterior Nero Noctis with Arancio Details

Race-Grade Cockpit

The cabin is pure STO theatre. Alcantara dominates the dash, door cards, and seats, while the exposed carbon structure and bright switchgear keep the mood unapologetically motorsport. Even standing still, the interior feels wired for a hot lap rather than a casual drive through the city.

Unfiltered V10 Drama

Beneath the rear cover sits one of the last great naturally aspirated supercar engines. No hybrid system, no turbochargers, no dilution. Just Lamborghini's 5.2-litre V10, visible through the structural framework and presented like a centerpiece. It is mechanical theatre backed by instant throttle response and a soundtrack the modern era is rapidly losing.
